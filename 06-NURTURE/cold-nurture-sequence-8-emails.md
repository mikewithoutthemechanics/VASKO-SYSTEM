# Cold Nurture Sequence (8 Emails)

## Audience
Leads who downloaded content, attended a webinar, or met at an event but are not yet sales-ready.

## Objective
Warm the lead over 4 weeks. Move from "aware" to "interested" to "ready for a conversation."
