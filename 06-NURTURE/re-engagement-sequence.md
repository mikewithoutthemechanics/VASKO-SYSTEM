# Re-Engagement Sequence

## Audience
Clients or prospects who went cold. No response for 30+ days.

## Objective
Re-establish contact with a low-friction, value-first message.

---

## Email 1: Day 1 - The Low-Stakes Check-In
**Subject**: Checking in (no ask)
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

It has been a while since we last spoke. I am not following up on the proposal. I am just checking in to see how things are going with [Company].

If you are heads-down on something, no need to reply. Just wanted to say hi.

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Email 2: Day 7 - The Value Add
**Subject**: Something you might find useful
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

I came across this [article/video/tool] and immediately thought of you.

[Link]

No pitch. Just sharing something that might help with [specific challenge they mentioned].

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Email 3: Day 14 - The Honest Question
**Subject**: Is VASKO still relevant for you?
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

I will be honest: I hate being the person who keeps following up with no response.

If VASKO is not a priority right now, just say "not now" and I will remove you from follow-up. No hard feelings.

If it is still on the table, let me know what is blocking you and I will work around it.

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Email 4: Day 21 - The New Hook
**Subject**: We just added [new feature/resource]
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

Quick update: we just launched [new feature / case study / template] that is directly relevant to [their industry/pain point].

[One-sentence description of what it is and why it matters.]

If you want early access, just reply.

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Email 5: Day 30 - The Break-Up
**Subject**: Last call
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

This is my last email on this topic. I do not want to be part of your inbox clutter.

If VASKO becomes relevant in Q[Next Quarter], just reply with "BACK" and we will pick up where we left off.

Otherwise, I wish you and [Company] all the best.

Best,
[Your name]
