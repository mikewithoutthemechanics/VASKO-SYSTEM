# Warm Nurture Post-Discovery (4 Emails)

## Audience
Prospects who have had a discovery call but have not yet signed.

## Objective
Reinforce value, address remaining objections, and drive to close.

---

## Email 1: Day 1 - The Recap
**Subject**: Recap of our call + next steps
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the call earlier. I enjoyed learning about [Company] and the challenges you are facing with [pain point].

Here is a quick recap of what we discussed:
- Your current close rate: [X]%
- Target close rate: [X+10]%
- Investment: R[amount]/month
- Timeline: 14 days to deployment

I will send over the formal proposal by [day]. In the meantime, if any other questions come up, just reply.

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Email 2: Day 3 - The Proof
**Subject**: [Similar company] got these results in 60 days
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

I was thinking about our conversation and wanted to share something relevant.

[Similar Company], who is in a similar position to you, deployed VASKO last quarter. Here is what changed:
- Close rate: 18% → 29%
- Deal size: R65k → R95k
- Onboarding time: 3 weeks → 10 days

That is what happens when your team has a system instead of a guess.

Let me know if you want to see their full case study.

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Email 3: Day 7 - The Objection Handler

## Email 4: Day 10 - The Close
**Subject**: Ready to move forward?
**Body**:
Hi [Name],

We have been talking for about 10 days. I want to check in: is VASKO still something you are interested in?

If yes, here is the fastest path forward:
1. Sign the attached proposal.
2. Pay the 50% setup fee.
3. Schedule kickoff for [date].

If no, or if the timing is not right, just say so. I appreciate your honesty either way.

Best,
[Your name]
