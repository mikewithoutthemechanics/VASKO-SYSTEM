# Cash Flow Management Rules

## Principles
1. **Cash is king**: Profit is opinion. Cash is fact.
2. **Plan for the worst**: Assume 20% lower revenue, 20% higher expenses.
3. **Keep a runway**: Always have 6+ months of runway.
4. **Invoice fast, collect faster**: Send invoices within 24 hours of deliverable.

---

## Invoicing Rules

| Rule | Standard |
|------|----------|
| Invoice timing | Within 24 hours of milestone |
| Payment terms | Net 3 (setup), Net 7 (midpoint), Net 14 (final) |
| Late fee | 1.5% per month after 14 days |
| Suspension | Work suspended after 30 days overdue |
| Escalation | MD notified after 14 days overdue |

---

## Expense Rules

| Category | Approval Required | Monthly Budget |
|----------|-------------------|----------------|
| Software/tools | Team lead | R5,000 |
| Marketing | MD | R20,000 |
| Travel | MD | R10,000 |
| Contractor hire | MD | R15,000 |
| Capital expenditure | Founder | R50,000 |

---

## Weekly Cash Flow Review

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Monday | Review outstanding invoices |
| Wednesday | Follow up on overdue payments |
| Friday | Update cash flow forecast |

---

## Monthly Close Process

| Step | Deadline | Owner |
|------|----------|-------|
| Reconcile bank accounts | Day 3 | Finance |
| Reconcile payments | Day 5 | Finance |
| Review expenses | Day 7 | MD |
| Generate P&L | Day 10 | Finance |
| Board report | Day 15 | MD |

---

## Red Flags

| Indicator | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Runway < 3 months | Emergency cost review |
| > 20% invoices overdue | Review payment terms |
| Churn > 10% | Emergency retention protocol |
| CAC > LTV/3 | Pause paid acquisition |
