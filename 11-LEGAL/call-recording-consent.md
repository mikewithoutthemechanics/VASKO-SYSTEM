# Call Recording Consent

## Call Recording Consent Policy

### Purpose
VASKO records sales calls for quality assurance, training, and service improvement purposes.

---

## Consent Language (Client-Facing)

> "For quality and training purposes, this call may be recorded. Do you consent to the recording of this call?"

**If Yes**: Proceed with recording. Log consent in CRM.
**If No**: Do not record. Proceed with call. Log non-consent in CRM.

---

## Consent Language (Prospect-Facing)

> "Hi [Name], before we begin, I want to let you know that I may record this call for my own training purposes. Is that okay with you?"

## Retention
- Call recordings retained for 24 months.
- Recordings deleted after retention period unless required for legal proceedings.
- Recordings stored on encrypted servers with access logs.

---

## Rights
- Participants may request access to their recordings.
- Participants may request deletion of their recordings.
- Requests processed within 30 days.

---

## Compliance
- POPIA compliant (South Africa).
- GDPR compliant (EU clients).
- CCPA compliant (California clients).
- Local regulations checked per market before deployment.

---

## Incident Response
If a recording is accessed without authorisation:
1. Immediate investigation.
2. Notification to affected parties within 72 hours.
3. Report to Information Regulator (if required).
4. Remedial measures implemented.
