# VASKO Pulse Template (SA ZAR)

## Overview
VASKO Pulse is a structured client health check conducted at Months 1, 3, 6, 9, and 12 to identify retention risks and expansion opportunities.

---

## Pulse Survey

### Section 1: Satisfaction
1. On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with the VASKO system?
2. On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to recommend VASKO to a colleague?
3. What is the one thing we could improve?

### Section 2: Usage
4. Which VASKO modules are you using regularly? (Brand, Pricing, Sales, Outreach, Proposals, Nurture, Website, Onboarding, Retention, Growth)
5. Which modules are you NOT using? Why?
6. How many hours per week does your team spend using VASKO?

### Section 3: Outcomes
7. Has your close rate changed since deploying VASKO? (Before / After)
8. Has your deal size changed?
9. Has your onboarding time changed?
10. Has your outreach reply rate changed?

### Section 4: Risks
11. Are there any upcoming changes that might affect your engagement? (Budget cuts, leadership changes, M&A)
12. Are you evaluating any competitors?
13. Is there anything frustrating you about the current system?

### Section 5: Expansion
14. Are you planning to expand into new markets?
15. Are you hiring more sales reps?
16. Would you be interested in additional modules or services?

---

## Pulse Scoring

| Score | NPS | Action |
|-------|-----|--------|
| 9-10 | Promoter | Ask for referral / case study |
| 7-8 | Passive | Identify improvement area |
| 0-6 | Detractor | Escalate to retention protocol |

---

## Pulse Process

| Step | Owner | Timeline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Send survey | VASKO | Day 1 of pulse month |
| Reminder (if no response) | VASKO | Day 7 |
| Follow-up call | VASKO | Day 14 |
| Score and categorise | VASKO | Day 21 |
| Action plan | VASKO + Client | Day 28 |
