# Escalation Protocol

## When to Escalate

| Situation | Trigger | Action |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Client dissatisfaction | NPS < 5 | Immediate escalation to MD |
| Payment overdue | > 14 days | Finance escalation |
| Scope dispute | Unresolved after 48 hours | MD mediation |
| Critical bug / outage | System down | Emergency protocol |
| Competitive threat | Client evaluating competitor | MD + strategy lead |
| Executive complaint | Any C-level complaint | MD response within 1 hour |

---

## Escalation Matrix

### Level 1: Project Lead
- **Scope**: Minor issues, questions, standard requests.
- **Response time**: 24 hours.
- **Resolution authority**: Minor edits, scheduling.

### Level 2: Department Head
- **Scope**: Process issues, medium complexity problems.
- **Response time**: 12 hours.
- **Resolution authority**: Re-work, timeline adjustments.

### Level 3: Managing Director
- **Scope**: Major issues, client escalations, competitive threats.
- **Response time**: 4 hours.
- **Resolution authority**: Scope changes, pricing adjustments, client recovery.

### Level 4: Founder
- **Scope**: Existential issues, major client loss, legal threats.
- **Response time**: 1 hour.
- **Resolution authority**: Final authority on all decisions.

---

## Escalation Process

1. **Identify**: Project lead identifies issue and categorises.
2. **Notify**: Inform client of escalation and timeline.
3. **Investigate**: Assigned owner investigates root cause.
4. **Resolve**: Propose and implement solution.
5. **Follow-up**: Confirm resolution with client.
6. **Document**: Log issue and resolution for future reference.
