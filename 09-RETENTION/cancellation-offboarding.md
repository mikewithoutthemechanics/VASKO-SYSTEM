# Cancellation and Offboarding

## Cancellation Policy

### Notice Period
- 30 days written notice required for all cancellations.
- Cancellation effective at end of current billing period.
- No refunds for prepaid months.

### Final Deliverables
Upon cancellation, client receives:
- All custom deliverables created during the engagement.
- Source files (Figma, Google Docs, etc.).
- Access to shared workspace for 30 days post-cancellation.

### Outstanding Payments
- All outstanding invoices become immediately due.
- Late payment penalties apply per payment terms.
- Work suspended until cleared.

---

## Offboarding Process

### Step 1: Acknowledge (Day 1)
- Confirm receipt of cancellation notice.
- Schedule offboarding call.
- Provide timeline and deliverables checklist.

### Step 2: Data Export (Days 2-5)
- Export all custom content to client-specified format.
- Transfer ownership of all deliverables.
- Provide access to shared workspace.

### Step 3: Knowledge Transfer (Days 6-10)
- Walkthrough of all system components.
- Documentation of any customisations.
- Introduction to replacement vendor (if applicable).

### Step 4: Final Invoice (Day 10)
- Issue final invoice for any outstanding hours.
- Confirm receipt of final payment.
- Close project in CRM.

### Step 5: Follow-Up (Day 30)
- Check-in call to ensure smooth transition.
- Offer feedback survey.
- Keep door open for future engagement.

---

## Offboarding Checklist

- [ ] Cancellation notice received and logged.
- [ ] Final invoice issued.
- [ ] All deliverables exported.
- [ ] Shared workspace access transferred.
- [ ] Knowledge transfer completed.
- [ ] CRM updated to churned.
- [ ] Follow-up scheduled.
- [ ] Exit survey sent.

---

## Retention Recovery

Before offboarding, attempt recovery:
1. Understand the real reason for leaving.
2. Address the root cause if possible.
3. Offer a compromise (reduced scope, reduced fee, pause).
4. Document the lesson learned.
