# Month 4 Retention Protocol

## Objective
At Month 4, the initial excitement has worn off. This protocol ensures the client continues to see value and does not drift into passive churn.

---

## Month 4 Actions

### Week 1: Pulse Survey
- Send VASKO Pulse survey.
- Track NPS score.
- Identify at-risk clients (NPS < 7).

### Week 2: Deep-Dive Calls
- For all clients: 30-minute check-in call.
- For at-risk clients: 60-minute rescue session.

### Week 3: Action Planning
- Create retention action plan for each client.
- Assign owners and deadlines.
- Share plan with client.

### Week 4: Execution
- Implement agreed actions.
- Track progress.
- Schedule next pulse.

---

## Retention Actions by NPS Score

### Promoters (9-10)
- Ask for case study or testimonial.
- Offer referral incentive.
- Propose expansion (new modules, new markets).

### Passives (7-8)
- Identify the one thing that would make them a 9.
- Deliver that thing within 14 days.
- Follow up with a satisfaction check.

### Detractors (0-6)
- Schedule executive escalation call within 48 hours.
- Conduct root cause analysis.
- Present recovery plan within 7 days.
- Weekly follow-up until score improves.
