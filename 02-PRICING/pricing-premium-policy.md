# Pricing Premium Policy

## When to Apply Premium Pricing

| Scenario | Premium | Justification |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| Urgent delivery (< 7 days) | +25% | Weekend work, reprioritised pipeline |
| Annual prepayment (ZAR) | +0% | No discount; locked rates |
| Annual prepayment (International) | -10% | Cash flow benefit passed to client |
| Multi-year engagement | -15% | Commitment discount |
| Competitive bid situation | -5% | Match competitor, add value |
| Referral from existing client | -10% | Relationship value |
| Non-profit / NGO | -30% | Social impact alignment |
| Pilot / Proof of Concept | -40% | Lock-in mechanism for full engagement |

## Price Anchoring Strategy

### Positioning
Always present the **highest tier first**. Anchoring makes mid-tier options feel reasonable.

### The "Prime Sweet Spot"
- Position Prime as the recommended tier.
- Core exists to make Prime look valuable.
- Pro exists to make Prime look accessible.
- Elite exists to make Prime look scalable.

## Discount Authority

| Role | Discount Limit |
|------|---------------|
| Sales Rep | 0% |
| Sales Manager | 5% |
| Managing Director | 15% |
| Founder | 25% |

## Discount Script
> "I can work with you on the investment. Given your commitment to [specific use case], I can bring the monthly engagement down to [X]. That is the best I can do, and I want to be upfront about that."

## Anti-Discount Triggers
- Never discount without asking for something in return (shorter contract, case study, referral).
- Never discount more than once in the same conversation.
- Never discount after the client has already agreed to the price.
