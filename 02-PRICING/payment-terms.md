# Payment Terms

## General Terms

### Invoicing Schedule
1. **Invoice 1**: Issued on signing. Due within 3 business days.
2. **Invoice 2**: Issued at midpoint review. Due within 7 business days.
3. **Invoice 3**: Issued on final delivery. Due within 14 business days.

### Accepted Methods
- EFT / SWIFT (bank transfer preferred)
- PayPal (international clients only, +3% processing fee)
- Credit card (corporate cards accepted for setup fees only)

### Late Payment
- Overdue by 7 days: Friendly reminder sent.
- Overdue by 14 days: 1.5% monthly interest applied.
- Overdue by 30 days: Work suspended until cleared.

### Currency
- ZAR clients: invoiced in ZAR.
- International clients: invoiced in USD or GBP per agreement.
- Payment date is determined by receipt date in our bank account, not client's transfer date.

## Retainer Terms
- Monthly retainers billed in advance.
- Auto-renewal unless 14 days written notice given.
- Hours do not roll over between months.

## Refunds
- No refunds on completed milestones.
- Cancellation mid-engagement: client pays for all work completed to date plus 20% cancellation fee.
