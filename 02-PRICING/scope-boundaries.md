# Scope Boundaries

## What Is Included

### Brand (01-BRAND)
- Brand identity document (1 deliverable)
- Category positioning statement
- Messaging hierarchy (3 levels)
- Voice and vocabulary guide
- Visual identity direction document

### Pricing (02-PRICING)
- Pricing sheet tailored to target market
- International pricing sheet (if applicable)
- ROI reference table
- Payment terms document
- Pricing premium policy

### Sales (03-SALES)
- 2 x sales deck variations (Core + Pro)
- 2 x discovery call scripts (Version A + B)
- Objection handling playbook
- Urgency mechanisms reference

### Outreach (04-OUTREACH)
- Cold email sequences per market (SA, UK, US)
- LinkedIn scripts (3 templates)
- WhatsApp scripts (2 templates)
- Outreach research checklist

### Proposals (05-PROPOSALS)
- Proposal templates per tier and region (4 variants)
- Proposal examples with filled data

### Nurture (06-NURTURE)
- Cold nurture sequence (8 emails)
- Warm nurture sequence (4 emails)
- Re-engagement sequence (5 emails)

### Website (07-WEBSITE)
- Homepage copy
- Tier page copy (4 pages)
- About page
- FAQ page

### Onboarding (08-ONBOARDING)
- Welcome pack
- Onboarding questionnaire
- Kickoff call agenda
- Configuration documents
- Testing protocol
- Go-live protocol
- Hypercare schedule
- Engagement protocol
- Staff adoption framework

### Retention (09-RETENTION)
- VASKO Pulse template (SA + International)
- Month 4 retention protocol
- Escalation protocol
- Cancellation/offboarding process

### Growth (10-GROWTH)
- Referral programme framework
- Case study template
- LinkedIn profile copy
- 90-day content calendar
- Demand generation plan
- Competitive moat strategy
- Adjacent vertical expansion plan

## What Is NOT Included

- CRM software licensing or configuration
- Email platform setup (Mailchimp, HubSpot, etc.)
- Paid advertising spend or management
- Custom software development
- Translation beyond English (unless agreed separately)
- Legal review of generated documents (we provide templates, not legal advice)
- Ongoing retainer work unless explicitly scoped

## Change Requests
- Included: Minor edits within 14 days of delivery.
- Excluded: New deliverables, additional sections, or scope creep after midpoint review.
- Change request rate: R1,750/hour or 3-for-1 swap within same section.
