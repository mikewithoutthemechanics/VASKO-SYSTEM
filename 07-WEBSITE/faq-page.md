# FAQ Page

## General

### What is VASKO?
VASKO is a complete B2B sales system. We deliver brand, pricing, outreach, discovery scripts, proposals, nurture emails, onboarding playbooks, and retention protocols—everything your team needs to sell consistently and close more deals.

### Who is VASKO for?
B2B companies with 10-200 employees that have outgrown ad-hoc sales processes but are not ready for enterprise CRM overhead. Typical clients include SaaS, professional services, financial services, and healthcare technology companies.

### How is VASKO different from a CRM or sales tool?
VASKO is not software. It is a playbook. We give you the content, scripts, and processes that make your CRM useful. Tools without systems are just expensive contact databases.

### How long does deployment take?
14 days for Core, 21 days for Prime, 30 days for Pro. We work in sprints, not marathons.

### What is the investment?
Core: R12,500/month + R5,000 setup. Prime: R22,500/month + R10,000 setup. Pro: R42,500/month + R20,000 setup. Elite: Custom pricing.

### Is there a free trial?
No. But we offer a 14-day satisfaction guarantee. If you are not happy with the deliverable, we refund 50% and part as friends.

## Implementation

### What do we need to provide?
A dedicated champion (2 hours/week for 3 weeks), access to your current sales materials, and honest feedback during the review phase.

### Do we need to change our CRM?
No. VASKO works with any CRM. We can also integrate with your existing stack if needed (Pro and Elite tiers).

### What happens after deployment?
You own the system. You can run it independently or continue on a retainer for updates, coaching, and strategic reviews.

### Can we upgrade later?
Yes. Upgrade from Core to Prime or Pro at any time. Setup fees are pro-rated.
