# About Page

## Our Story

VASKO was built from frustration.

Frustration with sales teams that have great products but broken processes. Frustration with consultants who charge R50,000 for a deck that gets ignored. Frustration with software vendors who sell tools but ignore the human side of selling.

We decided to do something different.

We built a system that combines the precision of a Tier-1 strategy firm with the speed of a startup. No bloated retainers. No 6-month engagements. No deliverables that sit on a shelf.

Just a complete sales system that your team actually uses.

## Our Philosophy

### Systems Over Heroes
The best sales team is not the one with the best reps. It is the one with the best system. Heroes leave. Systems scale.

### Speed Over Perfection
We would rather ship a 90% solution in 14 days than a 100% solution in 14 weeks. Speed is a feature.

### Evidence Over Opinion
Everything we build is tested. We track reply rates, close rates, deal size, and onboarding time. If it does not move the needle, we change it.

### Integrity Over Hype
We do not oversell. We do not use fake scarcity. We do not promise results we cannot guarantee. We tell you what is possible, what is hard, and what we will not do.
