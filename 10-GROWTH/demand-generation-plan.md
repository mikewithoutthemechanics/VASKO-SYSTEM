# Demand Generation Plan

## Objective
Generate 50 qualified leads per month through a combination of content, outreach, and events.

---

## Channels

### 1. Content Marketing
- **Blog**: 2 articles per month on B2B sales topics.
- **LinkedIn**: 3 posts per week (see content calendar).
- **Email Newsletter**: Monthly digest of insights and case studies.
- **Webinars**: Quarterly webinar on sales system topics.

### 2. Outreach
- **Cold Email**: 100 emails/week to qualified ICPs.
- **LinkedIn**: 50 connection requests/week, 20 DMs/week.
- **WhatsApp**: 20 warm leads/week.

### 3. Events
- **Virtual**: Quarterly webinar (target: 100 attendees).
- **In-person**: Monthly breakfast / roundtable (target: 15 attendees).
- **Partnerships**: Co-host events with complementary vendors.

### 4. Referrals
- **VASKO Network**: 1.5 referrals per client per quarter.
- **Partner referrals**: 5 referrals per partner per quarter.

---

## Lead Qualification

### ICP Definition
- Industry: SaaS, professional services, financial services, healthcare tech.
- Size: 10-200 employees.
- Location: SA, UK, EU, US.
- Role: Head of Sales, CRO, VP Sales, Founder.

### Qualification Criteria
- Has a sales team (not solo founder).
- Has a product-market fit (not pre-launch).
- Has budget for sales improvement (R12,500+/month).
- Has decision-maker access.
