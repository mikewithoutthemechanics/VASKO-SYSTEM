# LinkedIn Content Calendar (90 Days)

## Content Pillars
1. **Education**: Sales tips, frameworks, insights.
2. **Proof**: Case studies, testimonials, results.
3. **Opinion**: Hot takes on sales trends, contrarian views.
4. **Personal**: Behind-the-scenes, lessons learned.

## Engagement Rules
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours.
- Tag relevant people and companies.
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per post.
- Post between 08:00-09:00 and 17:00-18:00 SAST.
- Mix formats: carousel, video, text, article, poll.
