# Competitive Moat Strategy

## Objective
Build defensible advantages that make VASKO the default choice for B2B sales systems.

---

## Moats

### 1. Playbook Depth
**Advantage**: 500+ real sales conversations refined into templates.
**Defense**: This is not copyable. It takes years of field testing.

### 2. Speed to Value
**Advantage**: 14-day deployment vs 3-6 month consulting engagements.
**Defense**: Speed is a structural advantage. We have optimised our process for speed without sacrificing quality.

### 3. Integration
**Advantage**: VASKO works with any CRM, email tool, or workflow.
**Defense**: We are platform-agnostic. Competitors often tie you to their tech stack.

### 4. Local Intelligence
**Advantage**: SA market knowledge with international readiness.
**Defense**: Local context (POPIA, ZAR pricing, SA business culture) is hard for global competitors to replicate.

### 5. Community
**Advantage**: VASKO Network of clients sharing best practices.
**Defense**: Network effects create switching costs.

## Threat Analysis
