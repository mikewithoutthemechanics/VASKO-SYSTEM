# Adjacent Vertical Expansion

## Objective
Identify and validate adjacent verticals where VASKO's core competencies apply with minimal customisation.

---

## Core Competencies

| Competency | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| B2B sales process design | Mapping, optimising, and documenting sales workflows |
| Playbook development | Creating templates, scripts, and guides |
| Outreach strategy | Building sequences that get replies |
| Pricing strategy | Value-based pricing for professional services |
| Sales training | Coaching reps on process adoption |
| CRM optimisation | Configuring tools to support the system |

---

## Adjacent Verticals

### Vertical 1: Managed Service Providers (MSPs)
- **Why**: MSPs sell services, not products. Same sales motion as VASKO.
- **Customisation needed**: Service-specific onboarding playbooks.
- **Market size**: Large (thousands of MSPs globally).
- **Entry strategy**: Partner with MSP associations.

### Vertical 2: Consulting Firms
- **Why**: Consulting is expertise sold through relationships. Same need for credibility and proposal consistency.
- **Customisation needed**: Proposal templates, scope boundaries.
- **Market size**: Medium.
- **Entry strategy**: Direct outreach to boutique firms.

### Vertical 3: Recruitment Agencies
- **Why**: Agencies sell people. Need for consistent client communication and candidate nurture.
- **Customisation needed**: Client-facing and candidate-facing variants.
- **Market size**: Large.
- **Entry strategy**: Case study from a recruitment client.

### Vertical 4: Financial Advisors / Wealth Management
- **Why**: High-value B2B relationships. Need for trust-based outreach and compliance-aware communication.
- **Customisation needed**: Compliance templates, regulatory language.
- **Market size**: Medium.
- **Entry strategy**: Regulatory compliance angle.

### Vertical 5: Legal Services
- **Why**: Professional services with long sales cycles and high deal values.
- **Customisation needed**: Industry-specific language, confidentiality protocols.
- **Market size**: Medium.
- **Entry strategy**: Partner with legal tech vendors.

---

## Expansion Criteria

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Market size | > 10,000 potential clients |
| Willingness to pay | > R10,000/month |
| Sales cycle | < 6 months |
| Customisation required | < 20% of core system |

---

## Go-to-Market Plan

### Phase 1: Validation (Month 1-2)
- Interview 10 prospects in target vertical.
- Build one vertical-specific module.
- Test with 1-2 pilot clients.

### Phase 2: Launch (Month 3-4)
- Create vertical landing page.
- Run targeted outreach campaign.
- Publish vertical-specific case study.

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 5-6)
- Hire vertical-specific sales rep.
- Build partner channel.
- Expand module library.
