# Referral Programme - VASKO Network

## Programme Overview
Turn satisfied clients into your best sales channel. The VASKO Network rewards clients who refer new business.

---

## How It Works

### Referral Flow
1. Client refers a contact via email, LinkedIn, or referral form.
2. VASKO reaches out to the referral within 24 hours.
3. If the referral becomes a paying client, the referrer receives a reward.

### Reward Structure

| Tier | Referral Value | Reward |
|------|---------------|--------|
| Bronze | R50,000 - R100,000 | R5,000 credit or equivalent value |
| Silver | R100,001 - R250,000 | R15,000 credit or equivalent value |
| Gold | R250,001+ | R25,000 credit + case study feature |

---

## Referral Agreement

### Terms
- Referrer must be an active VASKO client (not churned).
- Referral must be a new client (not previously in our CRM).
- Referral must convert to paid engagement within 90 days.
- Reward paid within 30 days of client's first payment.

### What is NOT Eligible
- Existing clients who expand their engagement.
- Leads generated from VASKO marketing.
- Competitor referrals.
- Any referral that results in a discount > 15%.

## Referral Kit

### What We Provide
- Referral email template (personalised).
- LinkedIn share image.
- One-page VASKO overview PDF.
- Unique referral link (tracking enabled).

### What You Say
> "I have been working with VASKO on our sales system. If you know anyone who is struggling with their sales process, I would be happy to make an introduction. Full disclosure: I get a reward if they become a client. But I would only refer them if I genuinely think VASKO can help."
