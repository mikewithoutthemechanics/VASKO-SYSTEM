# Brand Identity

## Mission
To transform B2B sales organisations from reactive pitch factories into strategic revenue engines that sell outcomes, not features.

## Vision
Every sales team operates with the precision of a Tier-1 strategy firm—compressed into 90-day sprints.

## Core Values

| Value | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| Precision | Every touchpoint is intentional. No fluff. |
| Evidence | Decisions are grounded in data, not gut feel. |
| Speed | Move from insight to execution in days, not quarters. |
| Integrity | We sell the truth, even when the truth is uncomfortable. |

## Brand Personality
- **Tone**: Direct, confident, unapologetically South African where applicable, but internationally sharp.
- **Archetype**: The Strategist + The Operator.
- **Communication Style**: Short sentences. Bullet over prose. Active voice. No jargon unless defined.

## Tagline Options
1. "Sell smarter. Close faster."
2. "Revenue engineering for B2B teams."
3. "From pipeline to predictable growth."
