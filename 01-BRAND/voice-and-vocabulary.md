# Voice and Vocabulary

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Confident | We know what works because we have tested it. |
| Precise | Every word earns its place. No filler. |
| Approachable | We are not above the client; we are alongside them. |
| Results-Driven | Outcomes over opinions. |

## Vocabulary Guide

### Use These Words
- Deploy, implement, execute, close, pipeline, conversion, revenue, predictable, system, playbook, template, sequence, objection, qualification, discovery, proposal, onboarding, retention, referral.

### Avoid These Words
- Synergy, leverage, disrupt, innovative (without proof), best-in-class, thought-leadership (as a noun), "we think", "maybe", "hopefully".

## Sentence Patterns

### Strong Openers
- "The fastest way to close more deals is..."
- "Most sales teams fail because..."
- "We built VASKO because..."
- "Here is what the data shows..."

### Weak Openers (Avoid)
- "In today's fast-paced business environment..."
- "It goes without saying..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "When all is said and done..."

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Cold email | Assertive, brief, value-first |
| Discovery call | Curious, structured, consultative |
| Proposal | Confident, detailed, outcome-focused |
| LinkedIn | Insightful, opinionated, short |
| Customer onboarding | Welcoming, clear, over-communicative |
| Retention check-in | Proactive, advisory, human |

## Punctuation Style
- Use em-dashes (—) for emphasis.
- Use semicolons to link related independent clauses.
- Avoid exclamation marks unless celebrating a client win.
- One sentence per line in templates where readability matters.
