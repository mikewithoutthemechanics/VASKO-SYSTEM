# Category Positioning

## Category Definition
VASKO operates in the **B2B Sales Enablement & Revenue Operations** category—specifically targeting mid-market SaaS and professional services companies that have outgrown spreadsheets but cannot justify enterprise CRM overhead.

## Positioning Statement
For B2B revenue leaders who need predictable pipeline growth without hiring a head of sales, VASKO delivers a complete sales system—brand, pricing, scripts, and nurture—packaged as a turnkey playbook, not a consulting engagement.

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Approach | VASKO Differentiation |
|------------|----------|----------------------|
| HubSpot Sales Hub | CRM + automation | We deliver the *content* inside the CRM |
| Gong / Chorus | Conversation intelligence | We build the *conversations* before they happen |
| Outreach / SalesLoft | Outreach automation | We write the *outreach sequences* that feed the tool |
| Traditional consultants | High-touch, slow, expensive | Turnkey system, immediate deployment, fixed fee |

## Blue Ocean Strategy
We do not compete on features. We compete on **implementation speed**. Our unfair advantage is that we have already done the 1,000 calls. The customer buys the playbook, not the advice.
