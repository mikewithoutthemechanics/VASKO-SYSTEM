# Staff Adoption Framework

## Objective
Ensure 100% of the sales team actively uses the VASKO system within 30 days of go-live.

---

## Adoption Phases

### Phase 1: Awareness (Week 0)
- **Activity**: Launch announcement + team training.
- **Goal**: Every rep knows what VASKO is and why it matters.
- **Format**: 60-minute workshop + email announcement.

### Phase 2: Trial (Week 1-2)
- **Activity**: Reps use VASKO in live sales activities.
- **Goal**: 80% of reps use at least one VASKO asset per day.
- **Format**: Daily standup check-ins + gamification.

### Phase 3: Habit (Week 3-4)
- **Activity**: VASKO becomes the default way of working.
- **Goal**: 100% of reps use VASKO for all standard activities.
- **Format**: Manager coaching + performance tracking.

### Phase 4: Mastery (Month 2+)
- **Activity**: Reps contribute to system improvement.
- **Goal**: Reps suggest improvements and share wins.
- **Format**: Monthly feedback sessions.

---

## Adoption Tactics

### 1. Mandatory Training
- All reps attend the VASKO workshop.
- Quiz at the end (pass mark: 80%).
- Certification: "VASKO Certified Rep".

### 2. Manager Buy-In
- Sales managers use VASKO first.
- Managers coach reps using VASKO scripts.
- Managers report on VASKO usage in weekly meetings.

### 3. Gamification
- Leaderboard for VASKO usage.
- Rewards for top users (gift cards, recognition).
- "VASKO Champion of the Month".

### 4. Easy Access
- VASKO assets embedded in CRM.
- Quick-access desktop shortcuts.
- Mobile-friendly templates.

### 5. Feedback Loop
- Weekly feedback form (3 questions).
- Monthly survey.
- Quarterly workshop.

---

## Resistance Management

### Common Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I prefer my own way" | The system works because it is tested. Your way is untested. |
| "It takes too long" | It takes 2 minutes. Without it, you waste 20 minutes reinventing it. |
| "I do not see the value" | Let us track your close rate before and after. The numbers will convince you. |
| "The templates feel generic" | They are starting points. Customise them. Make them yours. |

### Escalation
If a rep refuses to adopt after 30 days:
1. Manager coaching session.
2. Performance review with CSO.
3. Performance improvement plan.
