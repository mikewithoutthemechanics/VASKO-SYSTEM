## What Happens Next

### Day 1: Kickoff Call
- Meet your dedicated project lead.
- Review project timeline and milestones.
- Confirm stakeholder contacts and communication channels.
- Set up shared workspace (Notion / Google Drive).

### Week 1: Discovery
- Stakeholder interviews (sales, marketing, customer success).
- Review of current sales materials.
- Pain point mapping session.
- Competitive landscape review.

### Weeks 2-3: Build
- Content creation and template design.
- Weekly check-ins (30 mins every Friday).
- Draft deliverables shared for feedback.

### Week 4: Review and Deploy
- Final review session (2 hours).
- Revisions and finalisation.
- Team training session.
- Go-live.
