# Kickoff Call Agenda

## Meeting Details
- **Duration**: 60 minutes
- **Attendees**: Client stakeholders, VASKO project lead
- **Objective**: Align on project goals, timeline, and responsibilities

---

## Agenda

### 1. Introductions (5 mins)
- VASKO team introductions.
- Client stakeholder introductions.
- Role clarification.

### 2. Project Overview (10 mins)
- VASKO system walkthrough.
- What is included and what is not.
- Expected outcomes and success metrics.
- Timeline and milestones.

### 3. Discovery Deep-Dive (20 mins)
- Review questionnaire responses.
- Pain point mapping exercise.
- Competitive landscape discussion.
- Market dynamics.

### 4. Scope Confirmation (10 mins)
- Confirm deliverables.
- Confirm exclusions.
- Confirm change request process.
- Confirm communication channels.

### 5. Timeline and Milestones (5 mins)
- Review project calendar.
- Confirm key dates.
- Confirm review cycles.

### 6. Next Steps (5 mins)
- Send shared workspace link.
- Send calendar invites for weekly check-ins.
- Confirm first deliverable deadline.
- Assign project champion.

### 7. Q&A (5 mins)
- Open floor for questions.
- Address concerns.
