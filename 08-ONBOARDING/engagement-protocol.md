# Engagement Protocol

## Engagement Philosophy
We do not just deliver files. We embed ourselves in your revenue process until the system runs itself.

---

## Communication Cadence

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Participants |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Daily Standup | Daily (Week 1-2) | 15 mins | Project lead + champion |
| Weekly Review | Weekly | 30 mins | Project lead + stakeholders |
| Monthly Check-in | Monthly | 60 mins | Full team |
| Quarterly Review | Quarterly | 90 mins | Executive team |

---

## Feedback Loop

### During Build Phase
1. Draft shared via Google Docs / Notion.
2. Client reviews and adds comments.
3. VASKO revises within 48 hours.
4. Final approval required before next phase.

### Post-Launch
1. Weekly metrics review.
2. Monthly feedback survey.
3. Quarterly strategy session.
4. Annual system review and refresh.

## Change Management

### Requesting Changes
1. Submit change request via form.
2. VASKO assesses impact (time, cost).
3. Client approves or rejects.
4. VASKO implements approved changes.

### Change Request Categories
- **Minor**: Typo, wording change (< 1 hour). No cost.
- **Medium**: New section, additional template (1-4 hours). Pro-rated cost.
- **Major**: New deliverable, scope expansion. Requires new agreement.
