# Onboarding Questionnaire

## Section A: Company Overview

1. What is your company's core product or service?
2. Who is your ideal customer profile (ICP)?
3. What is your average deal size?
4. What is your current annual revenue?
5. How many employees do you have?
6. How many people are in your sales team?
7. What CRM do you use?
8. What email tool do you use?

## Section B: Current Sales Process

9. Walk me through your current sales process from lead to close.
10. What does your outreach look like? (Email, LinkedIn, calls, etc.)
11. How do you handle objections today?
12. What does your proposal process look like?
13. How long does it take a new rep to reach quota?
14. What is your current close rate?
15. What is your average sales cycle length?

## Section C: Pain Points

16. What is the biggest bottleneck in your sales process?
17. Where do you lose the most deals?
18. What do your reps complain about most?
19. What have you tried to fix these problems?
20. Why did those solutions fail?

## Section D: Goals

21. What does success look like in 90 days?
22. What is your target close rate?
23. What is your target deal size?
24. How many new reps are you hiring in the next 6 months?
25. Are you expanding into new markets?

## Section E: Content

26. Can you share your current sales deck?
27. Can you share your current proposal template?
28. Can you share your current outreach sequences?
29. Do you have any brand guidelines?
30. Do you have any existing customer testimonials or case studies?

## Section F: Stakeholders

31. Who is the final decision maker on this project?
32. Who will be our main point of contact?
33. Who else needs to be involved in reviews?
34. What is the best communication channel for your team?

---

## Instructions
Complete this questionnaire before the kickoff call. Be as specific as possible. The quality of the output depends on the quality of the input.
