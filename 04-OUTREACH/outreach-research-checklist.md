# Outreach Research Checklist

## Before You Send Any Outreach

### Prospect Research (10 mins per target)

| Area | Source | What to Find |
|------|--------|--------------|
| Company Overview | Website / LinkedIn | What they do, who they serve, recent news |
| Funding | Crunchbase / LinkedIn | Last round, date, amount, investors |
| Leadership | LinkedIn | Decision maker's background, tenure, interests |
| Pain Signals | Twitter / LinkedIn / News | Complaints, pivots, growth challenges |
| Tech Stack | BuiltWith / LinkedIn | Current tools, integration gaps |
| Hiring Patterns | LinkedIn Jobs | What roles they are hiring for (signal of growth or pain) |
| Content | Blog / LinkedIn posts | Topics they care about, gaps in their messaging |

---

## Per-Call / Per-Meeting Research

| Item | Source | Note |
|------|--------|------|
| Recent company announcement | News / Press releases | Tie into conversation opener |
| Prospect's recent post | LinkedIn / Twitter | Reference directly |
| Mutual connections | LinkedIn | Warm intro path |
| Industry trend | Google News / Reddit | Position VASKO as solution |
| Competitor activity | G2 / Capterra / News | Identify competitive gap |

---

## List Building Standards

- **Minimum viable research**: Company, role, 1 personalised insight.
- **Maximum list size per campaign**: 100 prospects.
- **Minimum time between touches**: 3 days.
- **Opt-out handling**: Immediate removal, no questions asked.

---

## Research Automation

| Task | Tool | Frequency |
|------|------|-----------|
| Company news alerts | Google Alerts | Real-time |
| LinkedIn post monitoring | LinkedIn Sales Navigator | Weekly |
| Funding announcements | Crunchbase alerts | Real-time |
| Job posting monitoring | LinkedIn / Indeed | Weekly |
| Industry trend reports | Gartner / G2 / Reddit | Monthly |

---

## Quality Gate

Before launching any outreach sequence, verify:
- [ ] Every prospect has a personalised opening line.
- [ ] No duplicate sequences within 90 days.
- [ ] Unsubscribe mechanism is functional.
- [ ] CRM fields are mapped correctly.
- [ ] Reply handling playbook is loaded.
