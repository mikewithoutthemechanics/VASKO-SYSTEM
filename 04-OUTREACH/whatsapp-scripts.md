# WhatsApp Scripts

## Guidelines
- Keep messages under 300 characters.
- Use line breaks for readability.
- Never send unsolicited voice notes.
- Always include an opt-out option.
- One CTA per message.

---

## Template 1: Warm Lead Follow-Up

> Hi [Name], following up on our call yesterday. As discussed, I am sending over the VASKO system overview. Let me know if you have questions. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 2: Meeting Confirmation

> Hi [Name], confirming our discovery call tomorrow at [time]. I will send a calendar invite shortly. If anything changes, just let me know. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 3: Proposal Delivery

> Hi [Name], your VASKO proposal is ready. I have attached the [Core/Prime/Pro] tier breakdown. Take a look and let me know your thoughts. Happy to walk through it on a call. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 4: Check-In (7 days)

> Hi [Name], checking in to see if you have had a chance to review the proposal. Any questions I can answer? Best, [Name]

---

## Template 5: Re-Engagement (30 days)

> Hi [Name], it has been a month since we last spoke. I know Q3 gets busy. If the timing is not right now, no pressure. Just wanted to keep the door open. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 6: Referral Request

> Hi [Name], quick favour—do you know anyone in your network who is struggling with their sales process? I am looking for 2-3 clients this quarter. If you have someone in mind, an intro would mean a lot. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 7: Value Add (No Ask)

> Hi [Name], came across this article on [topic] and thought of you. Thought it might be useful for your team: [link]. No strings—just sharing. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 8: Urgency (Genuine)

> Hi [Name], just a heads-up—we have one Prime slot left for August. If you want to lock in current pricing, I need a signed agreement by Friday. Let me know. Best, [Name]

---

## Template 9: The "No Pitch" Check-In

> Hi [Name], not selling anything today. Just checking in. How is the sales pipeline looking? Any wins to celebrate? Best, [Name]

---

## Template 10: Opt-Out

> Hi [Name], I want to make sure I am not spamming you. If you would prefer not to hear from me, just reply STOP and I will remove you. No hard feelings. Best, [Name]
