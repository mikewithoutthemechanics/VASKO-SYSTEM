## Immediate Actions (First 24 Hours)

1. COO / Deputy notified.
2. Business operations continue under authority matrix.
3. Key clients and partners informed by COO.
4. Team informed by COO.
5. Legal counsel engaged.

---

## 7-Day Plan

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | Emergency response, operations continue |
| Day 2 | Full team briefing, client communications |
| Day 3 | Financial review, cash flow confirmed |
| Day 4 | Legal review of agreements and liabilities |
| Day 5 | Board / advisory meeting |
| Day 6 | Decision on leadership structure |
| Day 7 | Communication to all stakeholders |
