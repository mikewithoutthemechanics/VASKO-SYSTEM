# Dependency Risk Mitigation

## Purpose
Identify single points of failure and create mitigation plans for each critical dependency.

---

## Dependency Register

| Dependency | Type | Risk Level | Impact if Fails | Mitigation |
|------------|------|------------|-----------------|------------|
| Founder | Person | Critical | Business halts | Backup Operator, incapacity protocol |
| Key employee #1 | Person | High | Delivery delays | Cross-training, documentation |
| CRM system | Technology | Medium | Data loss, process break | Regular backups, export policy |
| Email platform | Technology | Medium | Outreach stops | Redundant provider, SPF/DKIM |
| Bank account | Financial | High | Payments blocked | Second account, dual signatories |
| Domain name | Asset | Medium | Website down | Auto-renew, registrar backup |
| Hosting provider | Technology | Medium | Site down | Multi-region backup |
| Client relationships | Relationship | High | Revenue loss | Diversified portfolio, account mapping |

---

## Risk Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Founder Incapacity
**Impact**: Critical
**Probability**: Low
**Mitigation**: Backup Operator protocol, incapacity plan, key person insurance.

### Scenario 2: Key Employee Departure
**Impact**: High
**Probability**: Medium
**Mitigation**: Cross-training, documentation, retention incentives, 3-month notice for key roles.

### Scenario 3: Technology Outage
**Impact**: Medium
**Probability**: Medium
**Mitigation**: Redundant providers, regular backups, incident response plan.

### Scenario 4: Payment Processing Failure
**Impact**: High
**Probability**: Low
**Mitigation**: Multiple payment methods, 50% upfront policy, cash reserve.

### Scenario 5: Data Breach
**Impact**: Critical
**Probability**: Low
**Mitigation**: Security protocols, DPA, cyber insurance, breach response plan.
