# Backup Operator Agreement

## Purpose
This agreement defines the role, responsibilities, and authority of the Backup Operator in the event of founder incapacity or extended unavailability.

---

## Role Definition

**Title**: Backup Operator
**Reports to**: Founder (when available) / Board (when founder is unavailable)
**Term**: 12 months, renewable

---

## Responsibilities

### Day-to-Day Operations
- Maintain business continuity.
- Oversee client delivery.
- Manage team performance.
- Handle client escalations.

### Financial Management
- Authorise expenses within authority matrix.
- Monitor cash flow weekly.
- Ensure payroll and vendor payments.
- Escalate financial risks to board.

### Client Management
- Maintain client relationships.
- Ensure service delivery standards.
- Handle retention protocols.
- Escalate major client issues.

### Team Management
- Conduct weekly team meetings.
- Manage performance issues.
- Approve leave requests.
- Maintain team morale.

---

## Authority Limits

| Decision Type | Limit | Escalation |
|---------------|-------|------------|
| Operational expenses | R20,000/month | Board above limit |
| Client commitments | Within scope | MD approval for new scope |
| Hiring | Contractor up to R15,000 | Board for full-time |
| Pricing | Within approved matrix | MD for exceptions |

---

## Compensation

| Scenario | Compensation |
|----------|-------------|
| Active backup (ready status) | R5,000/month retainer |
| Activated backup (founder unavailable) | 10% of monthly revenue or R25,000/month, whichever is higher |
| Activation period | Until founder returns or successor appointed |

---

## Transition Protocol

### Activation
1. Family / legal representative notifies Backup Operator.
2. Backup Operator assumes authority within 24 hours.
3. Board notified within 48 hours.
4. Team notified within 72 hours.

### Deactivation
1. Founder returns to full capacity.
2. 30-day handback period.
3. Full authority transfer documented.
4. Compensation ceases.

---

## Confidentiality
Backup Operator agrees to:
- Keep all business information confidential.
- Not compete with VASKO during and after role.
- Return all assets upon deactivation.
