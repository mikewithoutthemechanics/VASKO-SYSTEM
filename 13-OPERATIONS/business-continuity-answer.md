# Business Continuity Plan

## Purpose
Ensure VASKO can continue operating during and after disruptions.

---

## Disruption Categories

### Category 1: Technology Disruption
**Examples**: Hosting outage, CRM failure, email platform down.
**Response**:
1. Switch to backup provider within 2 hours.
2. Notify clients of service interruption.
3. Restore services within 4 hours.
4. Post-incident review within 48 hours.

### Category 2: People Disruption
**Examples**: Key employee departure, founder incapacity.
**Response**:
1. Activate Backup Operator.
2. Cross-trained staff cover critical roles.
3. Documented processes ensure continuity.
4. Recruitment initiated within 7 days.

### Category 3: Financial Disruption
**Examples**: Payment processor failure, client non-payment.
**Response**:
1. Switch to backup payment processor.
2. Activate collections process for overdue invoices.
3. Reduce discretionary spending.
4. Board notified if runway < 3 months.

### Category 4: Reputation Disruption
**Examples**: Negative publicity, client complaint going public.
**Response**:
1. Legal counsel engaged within 1 hour.
2. Public response drafted and approved within 4 hours.
3. Direct outreach to affected clients.
4. Monitor social media for 72 hours.

### Category 5: External Disruption
**Examples**: Pandemic, natural disaster, political instability.
**Response**:
1. Activate remote work protocol.
2. Ensure all systems accessible remotely.
3. Weekly team check-ins.
4. Adjust delivery timelines as needed.
