# Early Adopter Positioning

## Objective
Position VASKO to attract early adopters who will provide testimonials, case studies, and referrals.

---

## Early Adopter Profile

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|----------|
| Company size | 10-50 employees |
| Revenue | R5M-R50M |
| Industry | SaaS, professional services, fintech |
| Geography | SA, UK, EU |
| Pain level | High (losing deals, inconsistent process) |
| Tech readiness | Uses CRM, open to new tools |
| Budget | R12,500+/month available |
| Decision maker | Accessible (founder, head of sales) |

---

## Value Proposition for Early Adopters

### What They Get
- Full VASKO system at 40% discount.
- Priority support and direct access to founder.
- Co-creation opportunity: their feedback shapes the product.
- Case study feature (if results are strong).
- Lifetime loyalty pricing.

### What We Get
- Real-world testing of playbooks.
- Testimonials and case studies.
- Referrals to their network.
- Product improvement insights.

---

## Pricing for Early Adopters

| Tier | Standard Price | Early Adopter Price |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| Core | R12,500/mo + R5,000 setup | R7,500/mo + R3,000 setup |
| Prime | R22,500/mo + R10,000 setup | R13,500/mo + R6,000 setup |
| Pro | R42,500/mo + R20,000 setup | R25,500/mo + R12,000 setup |

---

## Onboarding for Early Adopters

- Weekly check-ins with founder.
- 48-hour turnaround on revisions.
- Direct Slack channel.
- Monthly feedback sessions.
- Quarterly roadmap input.

---

## Selection Criteria

1. **Pain**: Must have a clear, quantifiable sales problem.
2. **Budget**: Must have approved budget and decision-maker access.
3. **Timeline**: Must be ready to start within 2 weeks.
4. **Commitment**: Must commit to 6-month engagement minimum.
5. **Feedback**: Must agree to provide weekly feedback.

---

## Outreach Script for Early Adopters

> Hi [Name],
>
> I am reaching out because [Company] is exactly the type of company we built VASKO for.
>
> We are looking for 5 early adopters to co-create the system with us. In exchange for 40% off and direct access to our team, we ask for weekly feedback and a case study if the results are strong.
>
> Are you open to a 15-minute call to see if this is a fit?
