# Proposal Core (USD)

## Document Structure

### Cover Page
- Company logo
- "PROPOSAL" header
- Client name and date
- Proposal reference: VASKO-[YYYY]-[###]
- Validity: 14 days

---

## Section 1: Executive Summary
- One-page overview of the engagement.
- Problem statement (client's words).
- Proposed solution (VASKO system).
- Investment summary.
- Timeline.

## Section 2: The Problem
- What is broken in their current sales process.
- Cost of inaction (quantified).
- Why previous attempts failed.

## Section 3: The Solution
- Overview of the VASKO system.
- How each section maps to their pain.
- Expected outcomes.

## Section 4: Scope of Work
### Included
- [ ] Brand identity and positioning
- [ ] Pricing strategy and sheets
- [ ] Sales decks (2 variations)
- [ ] Discovery call scripts
- [ ] Outreach sequences
- [ ] Proposal templates
- [ ] Nurture email sequences
- [ ] Website copy
- [ ] Onboarding playbooks
- [ ] Retention protocols

### Excluded
- CRM implementation
- Paid advertising
- Custom software development
- Legal document review

## Section 5: Investment

| Item | Cost (USD) |
|------|------------|
| Setup Fee | $3,000 |
| Monthly Engagement | $850 |
| Add-ons (if applicable) | As quoted |
| **Total (Month 1)** | **$3,850** |

## Section 6: Timeline

| Week | Deliverable |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Brand, pricing, positioning |
| 2 | Outreach, discovery, proposals |
| 3 | Nurture, website, onboarding |
| 4 | Retention, growth, final review |

## Section 7: Terms
- Payment: 50% upfront, 25% midpoint, 25% on delivery.
- Validity: 14 days from issue date.
- Amendments: Written only, signed by both parties.

## Section 8: Acceptance
- Signature lines for both parties.
- Date fields.
