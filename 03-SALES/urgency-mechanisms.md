# Urgency Mechanisms

## Principle
Urgency must be real, not manufactured. Fake scarcity destroys trust faster than no follow-up at all.

## Valid Urgency Levers

### 1. Implementation Window
> "We can onboard a new Prime client this month. After that, we move to a 6-week queue. If you want to be live by [date], we need to start by [date]."

### 2. Pricing Lock
> "Our pricing is locked for Q3. We review and adjust in October. If you commit before month-end, you lock in current rates for 12 months."

### 3. Capacity Constraint
> "We take on 4 new clients per quarter. We have 2 slots left for Q3. First come, first served."

### 4. Risk of Inaction
> "Every week you wait is another week your reps are sending inconsistent outreach. At your current close rate, that is roughly R150,000 in deferred revenue per month."

### 5. Competitive Threat
> "I noticed [competitor] just launched [initiative]. If you wait until Q4, you will be playing catch-up in a market that has already moved."

## Urgency Scripts by Channel

### Cold Email
> "We have one implementation slot left for August. If we are going to work together, I need a signed agreement by Friday so I can reserve the slot."

### Discovery Call
> "Here is the honest truth: if you are not going to make a decision in the next 2 weeks, I should not take up more of your time. Not because I am pushy, but because I respect your calendar and my own."

### LinkedIn
> "I am running a free workshop on [topic] next Thursday. Only 10 seats. I will DM you the link—first come, first served."

### Proposal Follow-up
> "The proposal I sent is valid for 14 days. After that, the investment needs to be re-quoted based on current capacity."

## Do Not Use
- "Offer expires in 24 hours" (unless true).
- "Only 3 spots left" (unless true and tracked).
- "This is your last chance" (unless true).
- Any urgency tied to a date that is arbitrary.

## The "Soft Close"
> "When would be a good time for us to reconnect? I want to make sure you have everything you need to make a decision."
