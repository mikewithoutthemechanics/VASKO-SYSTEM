# Discovery Call Script Version A

## Pre-Call Preparation (5 mins)

### Research Checklist
- [ ] Review their LinkedIn profile and recent posts.
- [ ] Check company website for recent news/funding.
- [ ] Identify their role and reporting line.
- [ ] Note any mutual connections.
- [ ] Prepare one personalised insight relevant to their industry.

## Opening (0-2 mins)

**You**: "Hi [Name], thanks for making time. Before we dive in, is this still a good time for 30 minutes?"

**Prospect**: [Response]

**You**: "Great. I want to be respectful of your time, so I will keep this tight. The goal today is to understand where you are losing revenue in your sales process, and whether VASKO is the right fix. If it is not, I will tell you and point you somewhere else. Does that sound fair?"

## Qualification (2-15 mins)

### MEDDIC Framework

**Metrics**
> "What does success look like for you this quarter?"

**Economic Buyer**
> "Who else would need to be involved in the decision to invest in a sales system?"

**Decision Criteria**
> "If you were to move forward, what would need to be true for you to feel confident?"

**Decision Process**
> "Walk me through how a decision like this typically gets made in your organisation."

**Identify Pain**
> "Tell me about the last deal you lost. What happened?"

**Champion**
> "If this is a fit, who internally would fight for this?"

## Pain Deep-Dive (15-22 mins)

**You**: "You mentioned [pain point]. Tell me more about that."

**Follow-up questions**:
- "How long has that been a problem?"
- "What have you tried to fix it?"
- "If you fixed it, what would change for you personally?"
- "How much is that problem costing you per month?"

## Demo / System Walkthrough (22-27 mins)

**You**: "Based on what you have shared, I think the [Core/Prime/Pro] tier would be the right fit. Let me show you exactly how it would solve [specific pain]."

**Demo flow**:
1. Show the brand positioning doc.
2. Show the pricing sheet.
3. Show a sample outreach sequence.
4. Show the proposal template.
5. Show the onboarding checklist.

## Close (27-30 mins)

**You**: "Here is where I think we are. [Summarise pain + solution + investment]. Does that make sense?"

**Next step options**:
1. "I can have the full proposal to you by Thursday."
2. "Let us schedule a follow-up with your CFO."
3. "I will send you the Core tier pricing and we can go from there."

**You**: "Which of those makes the most sense for you?"

## Post-Call Actions
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 1 hour.
- [ ] Log call notes in CRM.
- [ ] Update pipeline stage.
- [ ] Schedule next touchpoint.
