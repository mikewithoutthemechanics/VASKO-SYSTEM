# Objection Handling

## Framework: Acknowledge, Reframe, Resolve

### Structure
1. **Acknowledge**: Validate the concern without being defensive.
2. **Reframe**: Shift the context from cost to investment, or from risk to control.
3. **Resolve**: Provide a specific next step or proof point.

---

## Common Objections

### "It is too expensive."
**Acknowledge**: "I hear you. R22,500/month is not a small commitment."
**Reframe**: "What I want you to consider is the cost of not having a system. If your reps are wasting 10 hours a week on bad outreach, that is 40 hours of selling time lost. At R2,500/hour in deal value, that is R100,000/month in opportunity cost."
**Resolve**: "Let us run the numbers together in the ROI calculator. If the math does not work, I will be the first to tell you."

### "We have tried this before."
**Acknowledge**: "That is frustrating. And you are right—most sales systems fail."
**Reframe**: "The difference here is that VASKO is not a tool you configure. It is a playbook we hand you, built from 500+ real sales conversations. You are not buying software. You are buying the process."
**Resolve**: "Show me what you tried. I will show you why this is different."

### "We need to think about it."
**Acknowledge**: "Absolutely. This is a decision that affects your whole revenue operation."
**Reframe**: "Thinking is good. But thinking without a deadline usually leads to doing nothing. What specifically do you need to think about?"
**Resolve**: "Let us set a decision date. I will send you the full system preview by Thursday. If you have not decided by next Tuesday, I will follow up once—and then we both move on."

### "We do not have the bandwidth to implement."
**Acknowledge**: "That is the most common concern I hear."
**Reframe**: "The beauty of VASKO is that we do the heavy lifting. You do not need a project team. You need a champion who gives us 2 hours a week for 3 weeks. After that, we run the system for you."
**Resolve**: "Who on your team could be that champion?"

### "Your competitor is cheaper."
**Acknowledge**: "They might be. And that is fine."
**Reframe**: "Cheaper usually means either less scope or lower quality. We do not compete on price. We compete on implementation speed and playbook depth."
**Resolve**: "Let me show you what they are not giving you. [Show comparison chart]."

### "We are not sure if this will work for our industry."
**Acknowledge**: "Fair enough. Vertical fit matters."
**Reframe**: "The core sales mechanics—discovery, objection handling, proposal structure—are the same across every B2B industry. What changes is the language. We customise that as part of the deployment."
**Resolve**: "Let me show you a client in [similar industry]. Here is what they achieved."
