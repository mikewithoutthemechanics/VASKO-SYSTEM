# Discovery Call Script Version B

## Pre-Call Preparation

### Mindset
- This is a conversation, not a pitch.
- Your job is to diagnose, not prescribe.
- If they are not a fit, say so. Trust is long-term currency.

## Opening (0-3 mins)

**You**: "Hey [Name]. I am glad we connected. Quick context: I specialise in building sales systems for B2B teams. I am not here to sell you software. I am here to find out if the problem you have has a system-level solution. Sound good?"

## Context Setting (3-10 mins)

**You**: "Before I ask anything, tell me about your current sales process. Walk me through what happens from the moment a lead enters your pipeline to the moment they either sign or ghost you."

**Listen for**:
- Lead sources and quality
- Outreach channels
- Qualification criteria
- Demo / proposal process
- Handoff to onboarding
- Follow-up cadence

## Pain Identification (10-20 mins)

### The "Five Whys" Technique

**You**: "You said [specific pain]. Why do you think that is happening?"

**Prospect**: [Answer]

**You**: "And why does [that root cause] exist?"

*Repeat until you reach the structural cause.*

### Pain Scoring Matrix

| Pain | Severity (1-5) | Frequency | Business Impact |
|------|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Reps can not find content | | | |
| Inconsistent proposals | | | |
| Long ramp time | | | |
| Low reply rates on outreach | | | |
| No standard onboarding | | | |

## Value Mapping (20-26 mins)

**You**: "If we fixed [top pain], what would that unlock for you?"

**Follow-up**:
- "More deals? Better deals? Faster deals?"
- "How would that change your forecast?"
- "What would you do with the extra time?"

## Investment Conversation (26-29 mins)

**You**: "For a team your size, the system typically sits at [Core/Prime/Pro]. The setup is [X], and the monthly engagement is [Y]. The way to think about it is: if this gets you one extra deal per month, it pays for itself 5x over."

## Close (29-30 mins)

**You**: "I want to be honest: if what you need is just a CRM, I am not the right call. If what you need is a system that makes your team sell better, we should keep talking. What feels like the right next step?"

## Red Flags to Walk Away From
- No budget authority and no path to it.
- They want a "quick fix" for a systemic problem.
- They have churned through 3 consultants in 12 months.
- They cannot articulate what they are buying.
